    ### What should I order if I want to grill meat?

    **Naengsam (naengdong samgyeopsal, pork belly)**: thin-sliced pork belly, the kind you'd find in neighbourhood BBQ joints all over Korea. Ours is Duroc, cut 4.5mm and grilled on cast iron without foil. 600g $48.80++, 1kg $68.80++, extra 300g $28.80++. On weekdays you can order it from 3pm; on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays, all day.

    **Pasam (cast-iron Duroc pork belly with spring onion)**: 240g of Duroc pork belly from a cast-iron pan, served with pajeori, a tangy, peppery spring onion salad that cuts right through the richness. $48.80++

    **Jjusam (spicy webfoot octopus with pork belly)**: spicy marinated jjukkumi (webfoot octopus) in the middle, thin slices of pork belly around the edge, stir-fried together. $48.80++

    ### Not in the mood for pork belly?

    **Golbaengi-tang (Korean whelk hot pot)**: whelks, a favourite on Korean drinking nights, in a clear broth with enoki mushrooms, fish cake skewers and chilli. $48.80++

    **Kkomak bibimbap (Korean cockle mixed rice)**: seasoned cockles over rice, topped with gim (Korean seaweed) and sesame. Mix it all up and dig in. $19.80++, or $16.80++ for weekday lunch (Mon–Fri 11am–3pm, excl. Sat, Sun and PH).

    ### Craving a hot bowl of Korean soup?

    **Kkori gomtang (oxtail soup)**: oxtail soaked for 6 hours to draw out the blood, then simmered for 8 hours into a clear broth. $28.80++

    **Udae galbitang (big bone beef soup)**: a big bone-in beef short rib in clear broth. $24.80++

    **Dwaeji jjokgalbi-tang (Korean-style bak kut teh)**: pork ribs simmered into a clear broth. If you grew up on bak kut teh, think of this as its Korean cousin. $21.80++
