    ### What comes with the naengsam


    Nine side dishes on this particular day. Banchan is made fresh in the
    kitchen every morning, so the line-up shifts a little from day to day.


    Lettuce, ssamjang, sliced garlic, green chilli, and a dish of salt and
    sesame oil come alongside. The garlic and chilli are meant to go on the iron
    with the pork.


    ### How thick is the cut


    Cheongdamchae cuts naengsam at 4.5mm.


    Thickness is what decides how frozen pork belly eats. Cut thinner and it
    crisps fast but leaves nothing to chew on; cut thicker and it keeps its
    juice but loses the lightness that makes naengsam what it is. 4.5mm sits
    between the two.


    Look at the slices standing on edge in the tray and you can count the layers
    of meat and fat.


    ### No foil on the iron


    The pork goes straight onto cast iron.


    Foil pools the melted fat underneath the meat and steams it. You also lose
    the browned face that only comes from metal contact. It makes for more
    washing up, and it is still the right way round.
