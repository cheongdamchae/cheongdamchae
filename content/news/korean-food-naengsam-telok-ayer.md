    ### 청담채 냉삼은 어떤 맛인가

    고기에서 나온 기름은 철판 가장자리로 흐르고, 그 자리에 김치와 콩나물을 올려 같이 굽습니다. 이 이야기는 [냉삼 철판 — 삼겹살 기름으로 구운 김치와 콩나물](/ko/news/how-to-grill-naengsam/) 글에 따로 적었습니다.

    ### What is naengsam, and why do Koreans love it?

    Naengsam (pork belly, short for naengdong samgyeopsal) is what neighbourhood BBQ places in Korea served back in the 1980s and 90s. The slices are thin, so they cook the moment they hit the hot iron and the edges go crisp. Fresh pork belly is everywhere in Korea now, but plenty of Koreans still go out of their way for naengsam. It tastes like the dinners they grew up with.

    ### What does it taste like at CheongDamChae?

    The pork is Duroc and the cut is 4.5mm. That's thin enough to crisp up fast, with enough thickness left that you still get a proper bite. There's no foil on the pan, so the meat sits straight on the cast iron.

    As the pork cooks, the fat runs to the edge of the pan, and that's where the kimchi and bean sprouts go. We wrote about that in [How we grill naengsam](/news/how-to-grill-naengsam/).

    Wrap a piece in lettuce with a dab of ssamjang (Korean soybean and chilli paste). The banchan (Korean side dishes) come in their own bowls, simple and home-style.

    ### Where do Koreans in Singapore go when they miss home?

    We're not a big name, and you won't find a queue outside. Koreans find us by word of mouth. Some are a few days into a holiday and want a Korean meal. Some are on a work trip and want a dinner that feels familiar. Some have lived here for years and miss the BBQ place near their old home.

    If you work around Telok Ayer, you're welcome too. We're open from 11:00 to 23:30 every day, so lunch works and so does a late dinner after the office.
